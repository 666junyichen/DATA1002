# 项目说明

## 中文

项目名称：COVID-19 Economic Impact Exploratory Analysis

时间：2022

项目类型：Academic data analytics project

我的角色：Exploratory data analyst / visualization contributor

使用工具：Python, pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook

我负责的内容：

- 对清洗后的经济数据进行分组聚合和趋势对比。
- 生成可视化图表，用于展示 2019-2021 年疫情前后变化。
- 分析就业、公共交通、国际教育和机场客流数据与经济活动之间的关系。
- 对图表表达效果进行解释，包括编码方式、趋势、局限性和不确定性。

数据来源：

- Stage 1 cleaned economic datasets
- International education, public transport, employment, and airport passenger movement indicators

分析方法：

- Grouped aggregation by year
- Time-series comparison across 2019, 2020, and 2021
- Bar charts and line charts
- Visual communication evaluation
- Trend interpretation and limitation discussion

最终结果：

- 产出分组汇总表和多张图表，解释疫情期间不同经济指标的变化。
- 机场客流数据中，年度总客流从 2019 年 44,428,845 降至 2020 年 11,227,692，并在 2021 年进一步降至 7,901,444。
- 就业数据中，平均 underemployment rate 从 2019 年 8.33% 上升到 2020 年 10.56%，2021 年回落到 8.18%。

可以写进简历的量化成果：

- Built grouped summaries and visualizations across four cleaned datasets to communicate 2019-2021 pandemic-period economic trends.
- Quantified a passenger movement decline from 44.4M in 2019 to 7.9M in 2021 and linked the trend to travel disruption.
- Compared labour market indicators and identified a 2020 underemployment peak of 10.56% average underemployment.

## English

Project name: COVID-19 Economic Impact Exploratory Analysis

Period: 2022

Project type: Academic data analytics project

Role: Exploratory data analyst / visualization contributor

Tools: Python, pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook

Responsibilities:

- Built grouped aggregations and trend comparisons from cleaned economic datasets.
- Created visualizations to show pre-pandemic and pandemic-period changes from 2019 to 2021.
- Analysed relationships between employment, public transport, international education, airport passenger movement, and economic activity.
- Evaluated chart communication quality, including visual encoding, trends, limitations, and uncertainty.

Data sources:

- Stage 1 cleaned economic datasets
- International education, public transport, employment, and airport passenger movement indicators

Methods:

- Grouped aggregation by year
- Time-series comparison across 2019, 2020, and 2021
- Bar charts and line charts
- Visual communication evaluation
- Trend interpretation and limitation discussion

Final outcome:

- Produced grouped summary tables and visualizations explaining changes across economic indicators during the pandemic period.
- In the passenger movement dataset, annual total passenger movement decreased from 44,428,845 in 2019 to 11,227,692 in 2020, then to 7,901,444 in 2021.
- In the employment dataset, average underemployment increased from 8.33% in 2019 to 10.56% in 2020, then decreased to 8.18% in 2021.

Resume-ready quantified achievements:

- Built grouped summaries and visualizations across four cleaned datasets to communicate 2019-2021 pandemic-period economic trends.
- Quantified a passenger movement decline from 44.4M in 2019 to 7.9M in 2021 and linked the trend to travel disruption.
- Compared labour market indicators and identified a 2020 underemployment peak of 10.56% average underemployment.
